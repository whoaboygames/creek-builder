Bush Drift

Build a creek obstacle

Put rocks, logs and gates on a stretch of creek, then drive it, take photos of the ute on it and send it to your mates.

With fingers: tap the tab at the bottom to add a rock, log or gate. Drag to move it, drag round its ring to turn it, pinch to resize it. Tap empty creek to let go, then pinch to zoom the map. The menu at the top right has the rest, with a full how-to.

How to use this
  • This is a plan from above with heights marked. Look around in 3D opens the real thing in its own window; keep it open beside this one and it follows your changes.
  • The ute travels left to right. The top of the picture is the driver's left.
  • Add: click a rock, log or gate on the left, or drag it onto the creek.
  • Move: drag it. Arrow keys nudge 5 cm (hold Shift for 50 cm).
  • Turn: drag the round handle, or press R / Shift+R (hold Alt for 1 degree steps).
  • Resize: use the Size slider on the right. The height changes with it.
  • Stack: put one thing over another, select it and press Bring forward (]). It then rests on what is under it, lifted and leaning to suit, and its label shows how high its top ends up. Send back ([) puts it underneath again. Things at the same level stand through each other.
  • Remove: Delete. Copy: Ctrl+D. Undo: Ctrl+Z. (On a Mac, Cmd for Ctrl.)
  • Slide the view: two fingers on a trackpad slide it and a pinch zooms. With a mouse, the wheel zooms and dragging empty creek slides the view.
  • Colours: pale rock a tyre can climb, orange-brown is too tall to climb square on, dark mossy rock is a wall.
  • Gates are what the driver aims for: green is the easy one, blue is harder, black is the hard one.
  • Show ute puts a true-size outline of the ute on the creek so you can judge gaps. Drag and turn it like a rock.
  • Measure: press Measure, click two points. Esc clears it.
  • Location (at the top) picks which stretch of creek the piece sits on.
  • Your work is kept in this browser as you go. My pieces can save one as a small file, as a backup or to move it to another computer. Share makes a link with the whole piece in it.

How to use this
  • Add: tap the tab at the bottom, then tap a rock, log or gate. It lands in the middle of the screen. Slide the row sideways for more.
  • Select: tap it. Tap empty creek to let go. Where things overlap, tap the selected one again to pick the one beneath it.
  • Move: drag it with one finger.
  • Turn: drag round the faint ring around it.
  • Resize: pinch it while it is selected. For a gate, the pinch changes the gap between the poles.
  • Look about: drag empty creek to slide the map. With nothing selected, pinch to zoom. Two fingers moving together slide the map.
  • Stack: put one thing over another, select it and press On top. It then rests on what is under it. Under puts it underneath again.
  • The buttons in the corner delete and copy the selected thing, and put it on top of or under what it overlaps. The arrow at the top undoes.
  • With a log selected, Loose makes it a log the ute can push, roll and drive over. Tap it again to fix the log down.
  • The ute travels left to right. The top of the map is the driver's left.
  • Colours: pale rock a tyre can climb, orange-brown is too tall to climb square on, dark mossy rock is a wall.
  • Gates are what the driver aims for: green is the easy one, blue is harder, black is the hard one.
  • Your work is kept in this browser as you go. Share sends the whole piece as a link.
