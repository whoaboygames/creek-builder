Bush Drift

Build a rocky creek crossing, drive a ute over it, take photos of the ute hung up on your handiwork, and send the lot to your mates.

On a phone? It all works with fingers, held sideways. Driving: open a piece and use the on-screen controls; the Photo button stops the ute and lets you look about and take a picture. Building: tap the tab at the bottom of the map to add a rock, log or gate. Drag one to move it, drag round the ring around it to turn it, and pinch to resize it. Tap empty creek to let go, then pinch to zoom the map. The menu at the top right has Drive it, Share and a full how-to. Full screen: on Android, tap Full screen to hide the browser's bars. An iPhone does not let a web page do that; if you want it, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open it from the new icon.

How it works

  1. BuildPut rocks, logs and gates on a stretch of creek, on a plan seen from above. Stack them if you like. Six stretches to pick from.
  2. DrivePress Drive it and the ute is waiting just above your piece, in Low Range. See if it can be done.
  3. PhotoPress V at any moment. Everything holds still and you get a free camera to fly around the ute. C takes the photo.
  4. SharePress Share for a link with the whole piece inside it. Your mates open it, drive it, and can make their own copy to change.

Good to know

Your pieces are kept in your own browser, on your own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere: a share link carries the whole piece inside the link itself. Clearing the browser's data clears your pieces, so save the good ones as a file from My pieces in the builder. The game is a fair bit to load the first time (about 27 MB); after that it starts quickly.
